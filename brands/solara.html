Sparkling mimosa

Solara

By invitation.

Blood orange and stone fruit over a dry sparkling base, with no added syrup. Citrus forward, dry through the finish, and built to stay drinkable across a long session rather than peak on the first mouthful.

It sells into venues as an aperitif pour and into retail as a single-serve can. The format suits poolside, festivals and anywhere glass is awkward, and the liquid holds up served cold and straight from the can or over ice with a wedge of blood orange.

Produced and packed in New Zealand. Available now to venues, bottle stores, distributors and event buyers.
